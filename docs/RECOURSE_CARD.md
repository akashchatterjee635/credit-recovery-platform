# Recourse card: credit-recovery recommendations

## Intended use

Recommendations are feasibility scenarios for shadow-mode and trained analyst
review. They do not guarantee approval and must not be presented as financial,
legal, or credit advice without the required human and compliance review.

## Required subgroup evidence

Report feasible-recourse rate, median cost, median action count, median recovery
duration, solver failure, infeasible-within-horizon rate, plan instability, and
manifold rejection for age bands, gender where lawful, income quantiles,
education, employment, family status, credit exposure, and thin/thick-file groups.

## Stability and limits

Plan instability is the weighted, scale-normalized distance between plan targets.
Recommendation churn measures changes in action direction under small input
perturbations. Stop release when any predeclared governance threshold is breached.

Prototype constraints are low-confidence and have not been lender, legal, or
consumer-impact approved. Recommendations must not encourage harmful borrowing,
misstate lender-controlled actions as borrower-controlled, reveal proprietary
thresholds, or replace correction, complaint, and escalation processes.
